About MindScan

Project synopsis and academic context — MCA Project, GJUST Hisar, 2026.

Project Overview

MindScan is a machine learning-based system that utilizes Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to analyze social media data and predict mental health conditions. The system identifies emotional patterns, sentiment, and linguistic features in user-generated text to detect early signs of mental health issues.

Traditional methods of mental health diagnosis involve clinical interviews, psychological assessments, and questionnaires conducted by experts. This project offers a scalable, automated complementary approach using ML algorithms.
